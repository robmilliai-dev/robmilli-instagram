## When to use this, and when not to

Use it if:

- You have a product that exists today, or one you can finish in a weekend.
- You can name the specific person who would pay for it and the problem it solves for them.
- You have somewhere people already see you: an Instagram account, an email list, a YouTube channel, a community, even a group chat of peers.

Don't use it yet if:

- You don't have a product. Build the smallest useful version first. A 12-page guide that solves one problem sells better than a 200-page plan you never finish.
- The product is regulated: financial advice, medical claims, legal services, anything age-restricted. Payment platforms have restricted-business lists and will freeze payouts. Check Stripe's [restricted businesses page](https://stripe.com/legal/restricted-businesses) before you build.
- You need physical shipping, subscriptions with complex proration, or a marketplace of other sellers. Those need a full store (Shopify, WooCommerce) and are outside this kit.

## What it costs

Nothing upfront on the recommended route. Stripe, Gumroad and Lemon Squeezy all charge per sale rather than per month. As of late 2026, Stripe's standard US card rate is a percentage plus a fixed fee per charge; Gumroad and Lemon Squeezy take a larger cut but also handle sales tax as a merchant of record. Rates change and differ by country, so check [stripe.com/pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing), [gumroad.com/pricing](https://gumroad.com/pricing) and [lemonsqueezy.com/pricing](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing) before you pick. Chapter 02 shows you how to compare them on your actual price.

A domain is optional (roughly $10–20/year from any registrar). You can sell from a payment platform's hosted page or a free link-in-bio page on day one.

## Three rules that hold the whole kit together

1. **One product until it sells.** A storefront with nine products and no sales tells you nothing. One product with traffic tells you whether the offer works.
2. **The page talks about the buyer, not you.** Your story earns one short paragraph. The rest is their problem, what changes after they buy, and exactly what they get.
3. **No claims you can't back.** Don't invent student counts, revenue screenshots or testimonials. Platforms ban accounts for it, buyers refund over it, and in many countries it's illegal under consumer-protection rules. Chapter 03 shows you how to sell with proof you actually have.
