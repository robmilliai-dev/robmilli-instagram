# Start here: your 10-week plan to a one-person AI business

**What this does:** It walks you week by week from "I have an idea" to "I have a paying client." You sell a result people already want. AI helps you deliver it faster.

**What you'll have:**

- ✅ **After 30 minutes:** a clear one-line offer
- ✅ **After 4 weeks:** real conversations with buyers, and proof they want it
- ✅ **After 7 weeks:** a simple way to deliver it, and a fair price
- ✅ **After 10 weeks:** a proposal, a paid kickoff, and a referral ask

**What you need:** An AI chat ([Claude](https://claude.ai/) free works), a spreadsheet or notebook, and email.

## Step 1: Pick something you can check yourself

Too vague: "Custom AI strategy for anyone."

Good: "I turn a local contractor's FAQ and service list into a clean, ready-to-post FAQ page."

Why it's good: it's for a clear buyer, it's a real thing you hand over, and you can check if it's right.

## Step 2: Write your one-line offer

Fill this in:

```text
For [who], I deliver [what]
using [info they give me] within [how long].
It's done when [how they can check it], and it doesn't include [what's not included].
```

Can't fill in "it's done when"? Make the offer smaller.

## Step 3: Follow the weeks

1. [Weeks 1 to 4](01-weeks-1-to-4.md): your 30-minute first win, then talk to buyers.
2. [Weeks 5 to 7](02-weeks-5-to-7.md): build a simple delivery system and set your price.
3. [Weeks 8 to 10](03-weeks-8-to-10.md): send a proposal, start a paid job, ask for a referral.

Everything you need to copy and paste is in the [operating kit](templates/operating-kit.md): prompts, scripts, a tracker, and a proposal you can fill in.

## The stop rule

If you talk to 3 good-fit buyers and none of them have a real problem, **don't build it yet.** Ask what they actually pay for. Change your offer. Talk to more people.

## Good to know

- You're selling a result, not "AI". Clients care about the result.
- Don't paste client data into AI without their permission.
- Never make up results, quotes or examples.
- 10 weeks is a plan, not a promise. If you need more time on a step, take it.
