# Start here: get a boardroom's advice on your next big decision

**What this does:** Before you spend money or time on a big choice, AI plays your CFO, marketer, operations lead, skeptic, customer, risk checker, and future you. They argue it out. You see every risk on one page. **Then you decide.**

**What you'll have in 30 minutes:**

- ✅ Every strong objection to your idea, in one place
- ✅ The weakest guess in your plan, called out
- ✅ A small, cheap test you can run before going all in
- ✅ A written record of what you decided and why

**What you need:** [Claude](https://claude.ai/) or [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/). Free works for most people. Nothing else.

## Pick the right question

Good: "Should I hire a full-time support person in the next 60 days, try a contractor for 30 days, or wait?"

Too vague: "How do I grow?"

Write down who decides, by when, and the "do nothing" option too.

## Try a mini-council right now

Copy this into Claude. Fill in the [brackets]. Leave out private employee or customer details.

```text
Help me review a decision. Don't decide for me. Question: [the choice and the deadline]. Options: [A], [B], [do nothing]. Facts I know, and where they came from: [list, or "none yet"]. Things I don't know: [list]. Limits: [money, time, promises I've made].

Give me 4 short, different views: the money risk, the "can we actually do this" risk, how customers feel, and the strongest reason not to do it. For each one, say which fact it uses or call it a guess. Ask me 2 hard questions about the biggest guess. Make the best case against your favorite option. Suggest the smallest test I can undo if it fails. Don't make up numbers, quotes, laws or odds. End by asking me what I'll decide and when I'll check back.
```

You now have real objections and a test you can run.

## The one rule

**A confident argument with no proof behind it is a question, not a vote.** "Customers will happily pay 20% more" is a guess until you test it.

✅ **You're done when** you have: the question, the options, the best argument against, what you still don't know, your choice, who owns it, a deadline, and a date to check back.

## Good to know

- It's one AI playing 7 roles. It helps you think. It doesn't replace real customer feedback.
