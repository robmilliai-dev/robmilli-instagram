Most software never makes real money. The ones that do fix one painful problem for people who already spend money on it. AI makes building much cheaper and faster. It doesn't skip the part where you talk to buyers. This kit helps you find out fast, and cheap, if your idea has buyers.

## Five rules this OS runs on

1. **Sell before you build.** A waitlist with real signups, or better, pre-orders, before writing much code.
2. **Niche beats broad.** "Scheduling for mobile dog groomers" can win; "scheduling for everyone" competes with giants.
3. **Billing is a feature.** Subscriptions, failed payments, cancellations and refunds must work perfectly, from day one.
4. **Activation over acquisition.** Getting each signup to their first success matters more than getting more signups.
5. **Honest numbers.** Track real MRR and churn, and never claim results, users or revenue you don't have.
