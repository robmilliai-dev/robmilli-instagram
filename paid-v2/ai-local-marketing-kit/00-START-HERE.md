## Four rules the whole kit runs on

1. **Real beats clever.** Real photos, real customers, real prices, real service areas. Fake reviews, keyword-stuffed business names and invented addresses get profiles suspended and, in many countries, break consumer protection law.
2. **Ask everyone, the same way.** You ask every customer for a review, not only the happy ones, and you never pay or reward people for reviews. Filtering who you ask ("review gating") and incentives both break Google's rules.
3. **Answer fast.** Calls, messages, form leads and reviews get a response the same business day. Speed is often the difference between the call you got and the one that went to the next result.
4. **Check before you publish.** AI writes the draft. You check every fact (hours, prices, licenses, service areas, claims) before anything goes live under your name.

This kit explains platform rules in plain language as they stood at release. Platforms change their rules; when something matters (a suspension, a legal question), check the platform's current help pages. Nothing here is legal advice.
