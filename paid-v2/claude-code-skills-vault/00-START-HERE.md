One command installs a whole pack, or just the skills you want.

- ✅ A follow-up list, with the emails already drafted
- ✅ One of your documents rewritten in plain words

## How every skill in the vault behaves

All 35 follow the same rules, so you can trust them the same way:

- **Facts from your files only.** Missing information is marked [MISSING] or [CHECK], never invented.
- **Drafts, not actions.** Nothing is sent, published, paid or deleted. Outputs are saved to clearly named files for you to review.
- **Originals are never overwritten.** Edits and cleanups write new files; file moves get a dry run and a log.
- **Professional limits.** Legal, tax, medical and financial topics produce questions for a professional, not advice.
