# Start here: one idea, six posts

**What this does:** You give it one real idea. It turns it into 6 posts, each made for a different app. You stop staring at a blank screen.

**What you'll have in 30 minutes:**

- ✅ An X (Twitter) thread
- ✅ A LinkedIn post
- ✅ An Instagram carousel plan and caption
- ✅ A TikTok / Reels script
- ✅ A YouTube Short script
- ✅ A newsletter section

All from **one** idea. Only use the apps you actually post on. Two great posts beat six weak ones.

**What you need:** [Claude](https://claude.ai/) or [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) (free works), and the social accounts you already have. No scheduler needed.

## Try it right now (2 minutes)

Copy this into Claude. Fill in the [brackets] with something real from your work.

```text
Act as a tough content editor. My audience is [who you help]. Their problem is [problem]. I saw this happen myself: [what you saw]. My lesson is [lesson]. My proof is [what I can show]. I want readers to [one next step].

Give me two opening lines for a LinkedIn post. One starts with what I saw. One starts with the reader's problem. Each under 35 words. Then give me a 3-bullet outline. Don't add any stats, stories, quotes or results I didn't give you. If my proof is weak, call it an opinion. No hashtags. No sales pitch.
```

Pick the opening line that sounds like real life, not the one that sounds the biggest.

**Can't fill in "what you saw" or "proof"?** Stop and grab one real example first: a screenshot, a before/after, or a lesson you learned the hard way. AI can shape your idea. It can't make up your proof.

## What you need to prep (5 minutes)

- **One real idea with an example.** "Productivity" is too vague. "I stopped booking 3 calls in a row because I kept losing my notes" works.
- **A short brand note:** who you help, 3 words for your tone, words you never use, and the one action you want readers to take.

## Your first batch

Go to [the first-win chapter](02-first-win.md) and follow it step by step. It takes about 30 minutes for the writing. Filming and design take longer.

When you're ready to post every week, see [publishing](04-publishing-and-troubleshooting.md).

## The one rule

Every idea gets one **idea card**: the fact, the example, the lesson. Every post must match the card. If a post says something that isn't on the card, delete it or check it.

✅ **Week one is a win when** you have 6 drafts you'd stand behind, and at least one posted.

## Good to know

- Don't copy other people's content or make up client stories.
- Health, legal, money or safety advice? Get it checked by a pro before you post.
- Only use images, music and customer info you have the right to use.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The posts all sound the same | Tell Claude: "Make each one fit how people use that app." |
| Claude added a fake stat | Delete it. Tell Claude: "Only use facts from my idea card." |
| It sounds like a robot | Add 3 of your own phrases to your brand note and rerun. |
