# Start here: 80 prompts that tell you what to post next

**What this does:** No more "what do I post today?" These prompts help you pick ideas, write hooks, scripts and captions, reply to comments, read your stats, and pitch sponsors. You bring your know-how. The prompts do the heavy lifting.

**What you'll have in 30 minutes:**

- ✅ Your voice set up, so AI sounds like you (not a robot)
- ✅ 5 content ideas your audience actually wants
- ✅ One idea picked, hooked and ready to make

**What you need:** Any AI chat. [Claude](https://claude.ai/) or ChatGPT free works. A notes app.

## Try it now (3 minutes)

Copy this into your AI. Fill in the [brackets] with true info.

```text
Be a careful content editor. My audience is [who]. They're trying to [do what]. I know this because [what I saw, or say "my guess"]. I make [type of content]. My next step for them is [offer and link, or NONE].

Give me 5 questions these people want answered. For each one: their situation, the answer I'd need to check from my own experience, and one reason it might be a bad fit. Don't make up research, stats, reviews, prices or results. If you don't know something, write UNKNOWN. End by asking which one I can answer from my own experience.
```

Pick the one **you** can answer from real life. That's your first win.
