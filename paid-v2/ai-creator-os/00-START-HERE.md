You get a clear plan for what to post, a weekly system that makes a week of posts in a few hours, a way to turn followers into email subscribers, offers from free to premium, launches, brand deals, and a monthly check-in that tells you what to do next.

For coaches, teachers, experts, hobbyists going pro, and small brands. Works at 200 followers or 200,000.

**What you'll have in 30 minutes:**

- ✅ A one-page plan: who you help, the result you give them, and your 3 or 4 content topics

**What you need:** A free [Claude](https://claude.ai) account and a calendar app. The planner tool needs Python (free), but you can also do it by hand.

## The honest truth

Most creators don't make a living from content, and nobody can promise you followers or money. The ones who do build a business usually have 4 boring things: a clear audience, useful posts every week, an email list they own, and offers that fix a real problem. This system is built around those 4 things. AI makes the "every week" part easy.

## Rules the OS runs on

1. **Serve one audience with one result.** Everything you post should make sense to that person.
2. **Own the relationship.** Followers are rented; email subscribers are yours.
3. **Consistency over intensity.** A sustainable weekly rhythm beats a burst and a burnout.
4. **Honest claims.** Real results only, credited when they're someone else's. Disclose sponsorships and affiliate links. Never fake numbers, testimonials or scarcity.
5. **AI drafts, you decide.** AI does research, outlines, drafts and repurposing; your experience, opinions and stories make it worth following.
