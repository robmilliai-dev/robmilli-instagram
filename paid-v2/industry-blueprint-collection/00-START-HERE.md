# Start here: AI setups for 10 kinds of small business

**What this does:** It shows you exactly where AI saves time and wins customers in 10 common industries, and how to set it up safely. Each blueprint gives you the 6 jobs AI helps with first, what AI does vs. what a person still does, the tools, ready prompts, the rules to watch, a week-one plan, and what to measure.

**Two ways to use it:**

- **You run one of these businesses:** pick your blueprint and set up one job this week.
- **You sell AI services:** pick an industry, learn it, and use the blueprint for your offer. [Chapter 04](04-selling-blueprints-to-clients.md) shows you how.

**What you'll have in 30 minutes:**

- ✅ One AI job picked for your business
- ✅ Its time and money savings worked out, using your own numbers
- ✅ The first step on your calendar

**What you need:** A free [Claude](https://claude.ai) account. The savings calculator needs Python (free), or use the [worksheet](templates/workflow-roi-worksheet.md) by hand.

## Good to know

- No promise of savings or sales. The [savings calculator](code/roi_estimator.py) just does the math on your own numbers.
- The rules notes tell you what to check, not what the law says where you are. Health, legal, money and insurance businesses should have a pro check the setup.
