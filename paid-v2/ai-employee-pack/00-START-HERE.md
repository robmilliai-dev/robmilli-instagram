# Start here: your AI Employee Pack

**What you'll have in about 30 minutes:** your first AI "employee" saved in Claude or ChatGPT, doing one job well, with a quick check you run before anything goes out.

There are **8 ready-made AI workers** in this pack: support rep, executive assistant, bookkeeper-lite, content editor, sales follow-up, researcher, QA reviewer and ops coordinator. Start with one. Add the rest once the first one works.

**The one rule:** your AI employees write drafts. **You** hit send. They never send emails, issue refunds, move money or change records on their own.

## What you need

- [Claude](https://claude.ai/) or [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/). A free account works for testing.
- One real task you'd like help with. Remove names and private details first.

No code. No installs.

Not sure? Start with the **Content editor**. Everyone has something to rewrite.

## Step 2: Hire it (3 minutes)

1. In Claude, click **Projects → Create project**. Name it after the worker, like **Content Editor**. (In ChatGPT, use **Projects** or **Explore GPTs → Create**.)
2. Open the worker's prompt file from the table above. Copy the whole prompt inside the grey box.
3. Paste it into the Project's **instructions** and save.

**No Projects on your plan?** Paste the prompt as the first message of a new chat each time.

## Step 3: Give it a real task (5 minutes)

Open a **new chat inside the Project** and give it your task. Tell it who it's for, what you want, and any facts that must stay exactly right.

**No task handy?** Try this with the Content editor:

```text
Audience: customers. Goal: explain our support hours. Channel: email.
Facts that must stay: Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. local time. Urgent requests use our existing emergency number.
Draft: Hey everyone, we are changing hours now, reach out whenever and we can help right away.
```

A good answer fixes the "reach out whenever" problem and doesn't make up a phone number.

## Step 4: Check it before it goes out (5 minutes)

Ask yourself 5 quick questions. Each one is yes or no:

1. **True?** Every fact came from you. Nothing made up.
2. **On task?** It did what you asked, and nothing extra.
3. **Safe?** No private info, no promises you can't keep.
4. **Clear?** The reader will get it.
5. **Next step?** It's obvious what happens next.

**Any "no" on 1, 2 or 3? Don't send it.** Ask it to fix that part, or edit it yourself. The [quality rubric](templates/quality-rubric.md) has the full version.

## ✅ You're live when

You've saved one worker in a Project, it gave you a draft on a real task, and you checked it before using it.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| No Projects option | Paste the prompt as the first message of a new chat each time. |
| It makes up a promise, a number or a link | Don't use that draft. Give it the real facts and ask again. See [chapter 03](03-review-and-repair.md). |
| The workers get mixed up | Give each one its own Project. |
