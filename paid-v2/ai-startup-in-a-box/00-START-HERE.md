# Start here: test your business idea in 7 days, before you build anything

**What this does:** You set up one AI Project that works like a small startup team. It plays your research lead, your strategist and your toughest critic. It helps you find out fast if people will actually pay for your idea.

**What you'll have in 7 days:**

- ✅ A clear one-line idea: who it's for, and what problem it fixes
- ✅ 5 smart interview questions to ask real buyers
- ✅ Real conversations with real people, tracked in one place
- ✅ A first offer you can send
- ✅ A clear answer: keep going, change it, or stop

**What you need:** [Claude](https://claude.ai/) or [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/). Free can work. A few hours a week. No coding, no website, no ads.

## Step 1: Write your idea in one line

Fill in this sentence:

> "I think **[who]** struggles with **[costly problem]** when **[it happens]**. I could help with **[small result]** by **[how]**."

Example (made up): "I think owners of small cafes waste hours when supplier prices change each week. I could send them a weekly price comparison from their invoices."

Can't name the who and the when? Make the idea smaller first.

## Step 2: Run a 10-minute test

Copy this into Claude. Fill in the [brackets]. Leave out private customer info.

```text
Be a tough research lead, not a cheerleader. My idea: [your one line]. My limits: [time, money, where I am, who I can reach]. Don't make up market sizes, quotes, prices or interview results. Split what I told you from what you're guessing.

Give me: (1) the riskiest guess about whether people will act, (2) five interview questions about what people did last time, not what they think of my idea, (3) who to talk to first and why, (4) a sign that would prove me wrong, (5) one thing I can do today without building anything. Ask me up to 2 questions if my idea is unclear.
```

**Good answer:** asks what people did last time the problem happened, and tells you who to talk to.
**Bad answer:** says "huge demand!" with no proof. Tell it to try again.

Then send **one real message** to a real person. That's the whole system in small.

## Step 3: Set up the full team

1. [The 30-minute first win](02-first-win.md): fastest start.
2. [Setup](01-setup.md): build your AI Project with the operator prompt and the 5 role prompts.

Keep **one Project per idea**. Each chat gets one role.

## The one rule: AI writing isn't proof

- An interview script AI wrote = a draft.
- A real buyer telling you what they did last week = proof.
- Someone saying "yes" to a priced test = strong proof.

Tag every note: **Saw it**, **They told me**, **I calculated it**, or **Guess**.

✅ **After 7 days, you win if** you can name the buyer, what sets off their problem, what they use today, the best evidence against your idea, and your next small test. Then pick: **keep going**, **change one thing**, or **stop**.

## Good to know

- AI can't give legal, tax or funding approval. Ask a pro for big decisions.
- Don't upload other people's personal info without their OK.
- Menus move around. If you can't find "Projects", search your AI's help page for "Projects".
- Chats don't always remember each other. Save key decisions in a doc inside the Project.
