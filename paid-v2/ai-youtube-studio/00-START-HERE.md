# Start here: your AI writers' room for YouTube

**What this does:** Claude becomes your writing team. It checks if your idea is worth making, writes a title and thumbnail idea, outlines the video, writes the full script, and plans your shots. You just film and post.

**What you'll have in 30 minutes:**

- ✅ A video idea that's been tested, not just guessed
- ✅ A title and thumbnail idea that tell the truth
- ✅ An outline and an opening you can record today

**What you need:** [Claude](https://claude.ai/) (free works), a YouTube channel, and a phone to record. Your normal editing app is fine.

## The order (don't skip ahead)

Idea → test it → title and thumbnail → outline → full script → shot list → film → upload details → review.

Don't write a long script before you know the idea is good. A great script for the wrong idea is still the wrong video.

## Try it now: the 3-minute idea test

Copy this into Claude. Fill in the [brackets] with what you really have.

```text
Be a tough YouTube editor. My viewer is [who, in what situation]. They ask [one real question]. I can show [what I can film: a process, a thing, my screen]. The honest answer is [answer]. What I can't show or claim: [limits].

Give me: (1) why they'd click, in one sentence, (2) a promise I can really deliver on camera, (3) an opening I can film with what I have, (4) what's missing that makes this idea weak, (5) your call: make it now, research first, or skip. Don't make up search numbers or how popular it is.
```

- **"Research first"?** Good. Check your comments for real questions, or ask a viewer.
- **"Make it now"?** Check you can actually film that opening.
