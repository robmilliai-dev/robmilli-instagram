## When to use it, and when not to

Use it when you're doing business work that involves writing, organizing or thinking through options, and you want a strong first draft in minutes.

Don't use it as a substitute for:

- **A lawyer** on contracts, disputes, employment law or compliance.
- **An accountant** on tax, filings or anything you'll submit to an authority.
- **A licensed professional** on anything regulated in your industry.
- **Your own checking** of every fact, number and claim before it goes to a customer.

## Two rules that make every prompt better

1. **Give it real material.** Paste the actual customer email, the real numbers, your existing page. The vault's prompts ask for this because real inputs produce specific outputs.
2. **Never let it invent facts about your business.** Every prompt includes a rule against inventing numbers, testimonials or claims. Keep that rule when you adapt them.
