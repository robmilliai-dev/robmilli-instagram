# Start here: run your agency without the chaos

**What this does:** Your clients always know what's done, what's next, and who has to decide. You stop chasing missing files. You stop writing reports from memory at 11pm.

**What you'll have in 30 minutes:**

- ✅ One client tracked in a simple sheet
- ✅ Every task with an owner, a due date, and a clear "done" test
- ✅ One honest weekly report, drafted by Claude, checked by you, ready to send

**What you need:** A Google account (free) or the project tool you already use, and [Claude](https://claude.ai). No new software to buy.

## The one rule

Every promise gets **one owner, one place it lives, and one next step.** That's it. This kit just makes that easy.

## Your 30-minute first win

1. **Pick one real client** and one thing you owe them soon.
2. **Open a new [Google Sheet](https://sheets.google.com).** Click **File → Import → Upload** and pick [the tracker file](templates/project-tracker.csv). Choose "Insert new sheet".
3. **Add 3 tasks:** one you finished, one that's due next, and one that's stuck. Give each an owner, a date, and a "done when..." line.
4. **Open [the weekly report](templates/weekly-report.md)** and fill it in from your real notes. Don't have a number? Write "not available". Never guess.
5. **Paste [the report prompt](prompts/report-drafter.md) into Claude** with your notes. Claude writes the draft.
6. **Check every number against your notes.** Then send it.

✅ **You're done when** you have one true report sent, and a next step with a name and a date on it.

## Quick test before you go all in

Ask your client, your delivery person, and yourself the same 3 questions:

1. What's due next?
2. Who says it's approved?
3. What happens if something is late?

If the answers don't match, fix that first, in writing. Then put it in the tracker.

## Good to know

- Examples in this kit are made up. Swap in your real info before you send anything.
- Never paste passwords, payment info, or private health, legal or money records into an AI chat.
- If a client says "no AI", just use the templates by hand. They work fine without Claude.
- Keep each client in their own sheet. Don't share one sheet across clients.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The import made one big column | Re-import and pick **comma** as the separator. |
| Claude added a number you didn't give it | Delete it. Tell Claude: "Only use numbers from my notes." |
| You don't know who approves the work | Mark the task **Blocked** and ask the client today. |
