# Start here: run your business like a big company, alone

**What this does:** It helps you stop rebuilding your work from scratch every time. You get one clear offer, one repeatable way to deliver it, and AI doing the busywork. Less chaos, more done.

*"Billion-dollar" is about thinking big and using leverage. It's not a promise of income.*

**What you'll have in 30 minutes:**

- ✅ The one step in your work that wastes the most time, found
- ✅ A simple checklist that fixes it
- ✅ Your next move, written down

**What you need:** A free [Google Sheet](https://sheets.google.com/) or a notebook. [Claude](https://claude.ai/) helps for the weekly review. No subscriptions.

## Your 30-minute first win

1. **(5 min)** List the last 5 times you did the same kind of work for a customer. No customers yet? Use 5 tasks from your own week.
2. **(7 min)** For each one, write: what they asked for → what you needed → what you made → how long it took → what you had to redo. Don't know the time? Write "unknown".
3. **(8 min)** Circle the step that keeps repeating. Turn it into one line: "When **[this happens]**, get **[these things]**, make **[this]**, check **[this]**, then send."
4. **(7 min)** Try that line on one old job. What was missing? Add it to your checklist.
5. **(3 min)** Write your next step on [the weekly review sheet](templates/weekly-review.md).

✅ **You win when** you have one checklist that stops one repeat mistake. That's it. No software needed.

**Example (made up):** A consultant writes every kickoff email from scratch. Looking at 5 past jobs, the problem is always the same: they forget to ask for the client's deadline. The fix isn't fancy AI. It's one new question on the intake form and one saved email.
