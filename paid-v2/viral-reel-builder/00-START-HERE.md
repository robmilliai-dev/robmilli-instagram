# Start here: turn slides and a script into a finished Reel

**What this does:** You give it slides or photos, a short script, your voice and a song. A small tool turns them into a finished vertical video for Instagram Reels, TikTok or YouTube Shorts, with smooth motion, captions on screen, and music that drops under your voice. In about a minute.

Nobody can promise a video goes viral. This kit removes the two things that stop most people: editing time, and not knowing what makes people stop scrolling.

**What you'll have in 30 minutes:**

- ✅ The free video tool (ffmpeg) installed
- ✅ The demo Reel built, so you know it all works
- ✅ Your own Reel, made from 3 of your slides and a voiceover you record on your phone

**What you need:** A computer, Python (free), and your phone to record your voice.

Open [Chapter 01](01-first-reel-in-30-minutes.md) and follow it step by step.

## Three rules the kit runs on

1. **The first two seconds decide.** Most of the work is the hook: the first words and the first frame. Chapter 02 is the most important chapter.
2. **Only use what you have the rights to.** Your images, your voice (or an AI voice whose terms allow commercial use), and music licensed for social video. Copyrighted songs from your phone's library will get videos muted or removed.
3. **Honest claims only.** Don't show results you didn't get, invent numbers, or imply endorsements. Platforms and laws on advertising (including the FTC in the US and the ASA in the UK) apply to short video too, and audiences notice.
