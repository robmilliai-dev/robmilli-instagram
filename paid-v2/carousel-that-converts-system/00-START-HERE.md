# Start here: make an Instagram carousel people save and act on

**What this does:** You take one real question your customers ask. This system turns it into a clear 6-slide carousel with one simple next step at the end. No more "tips" posts nobody reads.

**What you'll have in 30 minutes:**

- ✅ A 6-slide carousel written and checked
- ✅ Slides built in Canva
- ✅ One clear ask at the end (save it, visit a page, or read a story)

**What you need:** A notes app, a free [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) account, and your Instagram. AI is optional.

## Try it now: a 10-minute draft

Write down a question someone really asked you. Then answer it as a checklist across 6 slides. Example (a kitchen remodeler):

1. **Cover:** "Before you ask for a kitchen quote, get these 4 things ready."
2. **Why:** "Quotes are easier to compare when every contractor sees the same plan."
3. **Thing 1:** "Measure the room. Write down wall lengths and ceiling height."
4. **Things 2 and 3:** "List what stays. Take photos of the pipes and outlets."
5. **Thing 4:** "Pick a budget range. Split must-haves from nice-to-haves."
6. **Ask:** "Save this for your walkthrough. Want our free planning sheet? Tap the link in our profile."

That already beats a vague tips post. Check every line matches how you really work.

## Pick one job for your carousel

| If you want | Make | Ask them to |
|---|---|---|
| People to come back to it | A checklist | Save it |
| Better leads | A "is this right for you?" checklist | Visit your offer page |
| Trust | A before/after with real proof | Read the full story |

**One ask per carousel.** Don't cram "save, follow, share, comment and buy" into the last slide.

## Good to know

- Some Canva templates are paid. Look for the little crown badge before you use one.
- Don't paste private customer messages into AI. Change the names or leave them out.
- No promise of reach or sales. Post, see what people do, then improve.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Slides have too many words | One idea per slide. Cut until it reads in 3 seconds. |
| Nobody takes the ask | Make it smaller and match it to what the post taught. |
