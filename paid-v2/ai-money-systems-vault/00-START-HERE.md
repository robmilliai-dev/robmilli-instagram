# Start here: 12 real ways to make money with AI

**What this does:** You get 12 business models people run with AI doing a lot of the work. Each one shows what it is, who it fits, what you need, a 30-day plan, how to price it, the tools, and the risks. Plus a quick quiz to pick the right one for you, and a calculator that shows what it takes to hit your monthly goal.

**What you'll have in 30 minutes:**

- ✅ Your top 2 models, picked with a simple scorecard
- ✅ The real math for each: how many sales it takes to hit your goal
- ✅ One model chosen, with day 1 of its 30-day plan on your calendar

**What you need:** A pen or notes app. The calculator needs Python (free), or do the math by hand.

## The honest truth

Nobody can tell you how much you'll make. It depends on your skills, your market, your effort and some luck. Most people don't hit full-time income fast. If anyone promises exact earnings or "passive income from day one", be careful. [Chapter 02](02-the-hype-filter.md) shows you how to spot hype and scams.

What this gives you: real models, real steps and real math. Pick one that fits, test it cheap, then grow it or move on fast.
