They die from bet size.

The rule the pros use: decide the most you'll lose on this trade before you decide how much to buy. Then: position size = max loss ÷ (entry − stop).

Example: willing to lose $100, entry $50, stop $47. $100 ÷ $3 = 33 shares. Not 100. Not "whatever feels right".
