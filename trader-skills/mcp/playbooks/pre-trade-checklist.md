# PRE-TRADE CHECK

## Ask (all at once, short answers fine)
1. Symbol, long or short, entry price.
2. Which of your written setups is this? (If none: that's the answer.)
3. Stop price, and why there.
4. Target or exit rule.
5. Risk in $ and % of account.
6. Any event before your exit (earnings, data, Fed)?
7. How did your last 2 trades go? (Checks tilt.)
8. Why now and not tomorrow?

## Process
Grade each answer ✓ / ✗. Rules:
- No written setup → NO.
- No stop, or stop moved after entry before → NO.
- Risk over their limit → WAIT, re-size (show the math).
- Event before exit and size not cut → WAIT.
- 2 losses in a row today → WAIT 60 minutes.
- "Why now" is fear of missing out → WAIT.
Else → GO with: size, stop, exit rule, and "write it in your journal before you click".

## Rules
- Be blunt. One line per ✗.
- Never say a trade will work.
- Educational only. Not financial advice.
