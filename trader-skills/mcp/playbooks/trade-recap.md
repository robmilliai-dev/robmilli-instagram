# TRADE RECAP

The internet is full of green screenshots. A recap that shows the red one next to it is the one people follow.

## INTAKE

Ask for at least one true detail. Without it, refuse politely and say why: posts with no real detail read like ads.

Good true details: "My bot bought NVDA at 9:41 and the stop hit at 9:58." / "Week: 11 trades, 6 winners, biggest loss $212." / "The viral strategy had 4 trades in 6 years." Paste output from `/account-stats --format json` if you have it.

## PICK THE SHAPE

| Shape | When | Bones |
|---|---|---|
| **One trade** | A single trade taught you something | What I did → what happened → the lesson → what I changed |
| **The week** | Weekly scoreboard | Numbers → best trade → worst trade → one rule for next week |
| **The test** | You tested a strategy or bot | The claim → how I tested → result incl. worst drop → verdict |
| **The mistake** | You broke a rule | The rule → how I broke it → cost in $ or % → the fix |

## RULES FOR THE POST

- **First line under 12 words and it must earn the click.** It shows before "see more". Lead with the number or the twist.
- **Show the loss.** Every recap includes at least one losing number, the worst drop, or the catch.
- **One idea.** If you have two lessons, write two posts.
- **Plain words.** No "alpha", "printing", "tendies", "to the moon".
- **Platform length:** X 1 post or a thread of max 5; Threads under 500 characters per post; StockTwits under 1,000 characters with $TICKER cashtags; LinkedIn 150 to 260 words, line breaks every 1 to 2 sentences.
- **Hashtags:** 0 on X/Threads/LinkedIn, cashtags only on StockTwits.
- **End with a question or a small offer,** not "thoughts?". Example: "If you want the rule, comment RULE." or "What would you have done at 9:58?"
- **Visual:** suggest one: the chart with entry/exit marked, the scoreboard block as an image, or a `/equity-video`.

## OUTPUT

```
POST (platform)
<post>

VISUAL
<one suggestion>

WHY THIS FIRST LINE
<one sentence>
```

## HARD RULES

- **No invented numbers.** Every number comes from the user or their data.
- **No profit promises** and no "you can do this too and make…".
- **No exact account balance** unless the user asks. Percentages are fine.
- Add "Not financial advice." on posts that describe a trade.
