# TRADE JOURNAL REVIEW

## Ask first
Paste trades (date, symbol, side, entry, exit, size, P&L, notes) or a broker CSV. Plus your written rules if you have them.

## Process
1. Numbers: trades, win rate, avg win, avg loss, profit factor, best, worst, P&L by day of week and by setup.
2. Rule breaks: list each trade that broke a rule, and what it cost. Total cost of rule breaks vs. total P&L.
3. Patterns: time of day, holding time on winners vs. losers (cutting winners early? holding losers?), trades after a loss (revenge?), size changes after wins.
4. The ONE pattern costing the most, with the $ number.
5. ONE rule for next week. Testable. ("No trades after 2 losses in a day.")
6. One thing they did well. Specific.

## Output
Scoreboard table · rule-break table · the one pattern · the one rule · the one win · "next review: <date>".

## Rules
- Under 20 trades: say the stats are noise and focus on rule breaks.
- No judgment words. Facts and dollars.
- Educational only. Not financial advice.
