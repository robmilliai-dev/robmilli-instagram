# REEL SCRIPT

Most trading reels die in 1.5 seconds because they open with "Hey guys". This writes the first line first.

## INTAKE

1. What's the video about? (A bot, a backtest, a mistake, a tool, a result)
2. What's the one proof you can show on screen? (A chart, a number, a screen recording.) **No proof, no script:** ask for one.
3. What should they comment? (Your keyword, e.g. BOTS)
4. Optional: paste 5 to 10 of your past reels with views. The prediction uses them.

## STEP 1: THREE HOOKS, THREE FAMILIES

Write one hook from each of three different families, then rank them:

| Family | Shape | Trading example |
|---|---|---|
| **Big number** | Lead with the specific result | "This bot turned $150 into $279k. I rebuilt it." (credit whose result) |
| **I tested it** | You did the work they won't | "I tested the viral RSI strategy on 10 years of data." |
| **Contrarian** | The common belief is wrong | "Win rate is the most useless number in trading." |
| **Mistake** | Pain they recognize | "I lost $4k because my bot had no off switch." |
| **Secret tool** | Free thing they don't know | "Claude can read your TradingView chart. Free." |
| **Versus** | Two options, one winner | "ChatGPT vs Claude: same chart, same question." |

Each hook: max 12 words, says the payoff in the first 3 words, works with sound off as on-screen text.

Run the 3-question test on each and show the result:
1. Would a trader stop scrolling for this? (specific, visual, a little surprising)
2. Is the payoff clear in 1.5 seconds?
3. Can the video actually deliver on it? (if no, it's clickbait: cut it)

## STEP 2: THE SCRIPT (30 to 45 seconds)

```
[0-2s]   HOOK (spoken) | ON SCREEN: <hook text, 6 words max> | B-ROLL: <proof shot>
[2-6s]   RE-HOOK: why this matters to them in one line
[6-30s]  THE MEAT: 3 beats max. Each beat = one line spoken + one thing on screen.
[30-38s] THE TWIST or HONEST PART: the catch, the worst drop, what didn't work
[38-45s] CTA: "Comment <KEYWORD> and I'll send you <exact thing>."
```

Rules for the meat: short words, one idea per line, a visual change every 2 to 3 seconds. Show the screen, the chart, the code running. Faces are optional; proof isn't.

## STEP 3: CAPTION

First line repeats the hook with a different angle. Then 2 to 3 short lines. Then the CTA keyword in caps. Then the honesty line ("Backtest, not live" / "Their result, not mine" / "Not financial advice"). 3 to 5 hashtags max.

## STEP 4: PREDICTION

If past posts were given: pick the 3 most similar by hook family and topic, show their views, and predict a range (low / likely / high) with one reason. If no data: say "no baseline" and skip numbers.

## HARD RULES

- **Never claim someone else's result as yours.** "This bot made X" means you credit who reported it.
- **No promises.** Never "you will make", "guaranteed", "passive income", "$X a day".
- **No "giveaway" or "$X into $Y" phrasing in the caption.** Instagram flags it for link restrictions.
- **The honest part is mandatory.** One beat shows the downside. It's what makes the rest believable.
- Educational only. Not financial advice.
