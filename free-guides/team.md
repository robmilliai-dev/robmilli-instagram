## Step 1: Grab one customer message (3 minutes)

Pick one real message you need to answer. **Remove names, emails, order numbers and addresses first.**

No message handy? Use this practice one:

*Customer: "My package isn't here. Can you refund me?" Facts: tracking says the label was made Tuesday. No scans since. Our policy: we check after 3 business days with no movement. Refunds need the owner's OK. Owner: Sam.*

## Step 2: Give your AI employee its job (3 minutes)

Open a new chat and paste this first:

```text
You are my support rep. You write reply drafts. You never send anything.

Only use the facts and policy I give you. Never make up dates, refunds,
fees, links or promises. If you don't know something, say so.

If the message is about legal threats, safety, fraud or private data,
mark it ESCALATE and don't promise anything.

Answer in this format:
STATUS: READY / NEEDS INFO / ESCALATE
REPLY: (friendly, clear, under 150 words)
CHECK BEFORE SENDING: (facts I should double-check)
NOT SURE ABOUT: (anything missing)
```

## Step 3: Give it the message (4 minutes)

Send this as your next message, filled in:

```text
Customer message: [paste it]
Facts I know: [what you've checked, or "none"]
Our policy: [the rule that applies, or "none"]
Who approves: [your name]
```

You'll get a ready-to-check draft in seconds.

## Step 4: Score it before it goes out (5 minutes)

Give the draft a quick check. Each one is a yes or no:

1. **True?** Every fact in the reply came from you. Nothing made up.
2. **On task?** It answers what the customer asked.
3. **Safe?** No private info, no promises you can't keep.
4. **Clear?** The customer will understand it.
5. **Next step?** It says what happens next.

**Any "no" on 1, 2 or 3? Don't send it.** Fix it or ask the AI to redo it. Otherwise, tweak and send.

**You did it if:** you have one checked, ready-to-send reply, written in a fraction of the time.

> Keep the send button. Don't connect AI to your live inbox for this test.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| It makes up refunds or dates | Reply: "Only use the facts and policy I gave you." |
| Replies sound robotic | Paste 2 replies you wrote before and say "match this tone". |
| It answers a legal threat | It should say ESCALATE. If not, paste the safety rule again. |
