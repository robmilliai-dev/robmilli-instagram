# Find Your 3 Trading Leaks

**Most traders don't need a new strategy.** They need to stop the two or three things that keep costing them money.

The trouble is, you can't see them from inside your own head. **A journal can.**

**When you're done, you'll have:**

- ✅ A trade journal that takes 60 seconds a trade
- ✅ The 7 leaks to look for
- ✅ A Claude prompt that reads your journal and finds your worst one
- ✅ One rule for next week

**Why this works:** every trader remembers their trades wrong. We remember the smart wins and forget the angry losses. Rows in a sheet don't forget.

## Step 1: Make the journal (2 minutes)

Open a new Google Sheet. Put these in row 1:

```text
date, market, side, setup, entry, stop, exit, size, followed_plan, mood, notes
```

Three things make it work:

- **Use 3 to 5 setup names.** "breakout", "pullback", "news chase". Same words every time.
- **Mood is one word, picked before you click.** calm, rushed, angry, bored, tired.
- **followed_plan is about the plan, not the result.** A loss where you did everything right is a yes. A win where you doubled your size is a no.

## Step 2: Log every trade the same day

Yes, the bad ones too. Those are the ones that pay for the journal.

## Step 3: Know the 7 leaks

| Leak | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| One bad setup | One of your setups loses while the others win |
| Plan-breaking | "no" trades do much worse than "yes" trades |
| Revenge trades | Trades right after a loss, same day |
| Sizing up | Your biggest positions are your worst trades |
| Overtrading | The 3rd trade of the day and later |
| Mood | "rushed" or "angry" trades |
| One bad day or market | Fridays, or that one stock you can't leave alone |

## Step 4: A real-looking example

We made a sample journal of 64 trades for a pretend trader with common habits. Overall: **lost 4.5R.** Here's what the review found:

| Group | Trades | Won | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| "News chase" setup | 15 | 20% | -8.9R |
| Did not follow the plan | 14 | 21% | -8.0R |
| More than 1.5x normal risk | 11 | 18% | -7.1R |
| Mood: calm | 40 | 45% | +8.6R |

The calm, planned trades **made money**. One habit, chasing news with extra size, threw it all away and more. This trader doesn't need a new strategy. They need to stop one thing.

## Step 5: Find yours

After 20 trades, copy your rows and paste them into Claude under this:

```text
Here is my trade journal. For each trade work out R:
(exit - entry) / (entry - stop) for longs, flipped for shorts.
Then show total R and win rate for each of these groups: each setup,
followed_plan yes vs no, each mood, each weekday, trades taken right
after a loss on the same day, and trades with size above 1.5x my usual.
Ignore any group with fewer than 5 trades.
Tell me my 3 biggest leaks by total R lost, and ONE rule for next week
that a camera could see me keep. Don't predict anything. Don't tell me
what to trade. Show your math so I can check it.
```

**Check the math.** Chatbots slip on arithmetic with long lists. That's the main reason the finished kit does the numbers in code and leaves only the coaching to Claude.

## Step 6: One rule, one week

Write the rule where you'll see it. Keep it for a week. Then run the review again and see if the leak shrank.
