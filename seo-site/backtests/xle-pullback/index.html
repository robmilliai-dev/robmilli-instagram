Does buying RSI(2) pullbacks work on Energy ETF (XLE)?

Short answer below, with every number. The rules were picked using 2010-2019 data only. Then we froze them and tested on 2020-2026 data they had never seen. That second test is the one that counts.

Verdict: it worked on data it never saw.
From 2020-01 to 2026-10, the RSI(2) pullback rules made money on Energy ETF (XLE): 27 trades, 63% won, +2.3% total with a worst drop of -4.0%.

Holding Energy ETF (XLE) the whole time returned +203.8% over the same years, with a worst drop of -60.6%. This strategy made less than simply holding, but with a much smaller worst drop. It is built to sit out the big falls, not to beat a strong bull run.

The exact rules

  1. Only trade when the 50 EMA is above the 200 EMA and price is above the 200 EMA (a strong uptrend).
  2. Buy when RSI(2) drops below 5 (a sharp short dip).
  3. Exit on the first close above the 5 EMA, or at a 2 x ATR(14) stop.
  4. Risk 1% of the account per trade. Costs of 0.05% per trade included. Entries on the next bar.
