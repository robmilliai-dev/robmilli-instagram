Does buying the dip work on Broadcom (AVGO)?

Short answer below, with every number. The rules were picked using 2010-2019 data only. Then we froze them and tested on 2020-2026 data they had never seen. That second test is the one that counts.

Verdict: it worked on data it never saw.
From 2020-01 to 2026-10, the index dip-buying rules made money on Broadcom (AVGO): 82 trades, 70% won, +149.2% total with a worst drop of -35.1%.

Holding Broadcom (AVGO) the whole time returned +1082.2% over the same years, with a worst drop of -48.3%. This strategy made less than simply holding, but with a much smaller worst drop. It is built to sit out the big falls, not to beat a strong bull run.

The exact rules

  1. Only buy when price is above its 200-day average.
  2. Buy when RSI(2) is under 20 and the close is in the bottom 25% of the day's range.
  3. Exit on the first close with RSI(2) above 70, after 10 bars, or at a 5 x ATR(14) disaster stop.
  4. Fully invested while in a trade (no leverage). Costs included. Entries at the next open.
