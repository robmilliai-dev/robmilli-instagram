Does a 20-day breakout strategy work on Russell 2000 ETF (IWM)?

Short answer below, with every number. The rules were picked using 2010-2019 data only. Then we froze them and tested on 2020-2026 data they had never seen. That second test is the one that counts.

Verdict: it worked on data it never saw.
From 2020-01 to 2026-10, the 20-day breakout rules made money on Russell 2000 ETF (IWM): 27 trades, 44% won, +1.1% total with a worst drop of -6.7%.

Sizing: 1% of the account risked per trade. "Total" is the account growth from this strategy alone. "Worst drop" is the biggest fall from a peak.

Holding Russell 2000 ETF (IWM) the whole time returned +83.0% over the same years, with a worst drop of -41.1%. This strategy made less than simply holding, but with a much smaller worst drop. It is built to sit out the big falls, not to beat a strong bull run.

The exact rules

  1. Buy when the close breaks above the highest high of the last 20 bars.
  2. Only buy when price is above the 200 EMA (trading with the trend).
  3. Exit with a 3 x ATR(14) trailing stop that only moves up.
  4. Risk 1% of the account per trade. Costs of 0.05% per trade included. Entries on the next bar.
